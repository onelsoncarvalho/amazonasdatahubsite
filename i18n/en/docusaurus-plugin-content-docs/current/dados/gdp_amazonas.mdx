# gdp_amazonas

Gross Domestic Product - GDP of Amazonas

### Description

This database has data on the Gross Domestic Product (GDP) of the State of
Amazonas over the years (from 2010 to 2021). Includes values ​​in R\$
1,000,000.

### Usage


```r
gdp_amazonas
```

### Format

‘gdp_amazonas’ A data frame with 48 lines and 21 columns

#### year

Indicates the year of GDP observation.

#### quarter

Specifies the quarter of the year

#### agriculture

Refers to the economic production of agricultural activities, including
cultivation, harvesting and post-harvest logistical support.

####livestock

Represents the economic contribution of livestock farming, covering the production
of meat, dairy and related support services.

#### forest_fishing_prod

Production from forestry (wood and non-wood products)
loggers), fishing activities and aquaculture (fish farming).

#### extractive_ind

Industries extracting natural resources, such as minerals and oil.

#### manufacturing_ind

It refers to the transformation of raw materials into finished products.

#### basic_infrastructure

Covers services related to electricity and gas generation,
water supply, sewage treatment and waste management.

#### vehicle_retail_repair

Includes all commercial activities related to the sale of goods and
services as well as vehicle repair services.

#### transport_storage_post

Covers services related to the transport of people and goods,
storage facilities and postal services.

#### accommodation_food

Refers to economic activities in sectors related to tourism,
such as hotels and restaurants.

#### financial_activities

Includes banks, financial institutions, insurance companies and
various financial services.

#### real_state_activities

Refers to transactions involving the purchase, sale or rental of
properties.

#### professional_activities

Covers a wide range of specialized professional services,
including consultancy and administrative support.

#### public_admin_defense_health_edu

Represents public sector spending on government administration,
national defense, public education, health services and
social security.

#### private_edu_health

Refers to educational institutions and healthcare service providers
operating in the private sector.

#### art_culture_sport

Covers activities related to entertainment, cultural events,
sports facilities and other service-oriented sectors.

#### domestic_services

Includes personal services provided in homes, such as cleaning or
personal care.

#### taxes

Represents taxes charged on goods and services included in
GDP calculations.
