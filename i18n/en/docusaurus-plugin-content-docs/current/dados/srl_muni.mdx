# srl_muni

Physical Literacy and Reading Performance
children from schools in Amazonas: an association study

### Description

A database with the aim of investigating the association between
Physical Literacy and Reading Performance of Amazonian schoolchildren.
Method: This is an association, cross-sectional and approach study.
quantitative carried out with students from schools in Amazonas (n=100;
aged between 10 and 13 years old) from 3 public schools in the municipality of Borba
(Amazon).

### Usage


```r
srl_muni
```

### Format

‘srl_muni’ A data frame with 100 rows and 17 columns:

####school

School where the observation was carried out

#### Fri

Sex - 1 for Male and 2 for Female

#### age

Age in years

#### weight_kg

Weight in kilograms (kg)

#### height_m

Height in meters (m)

#### bmi

Body Mass Index (BMI) - Body Mass Index (BMI)

#### bmi_status

Categorical description of Body Mass Index (BMI)

#### phv

Peak Height Velocity (PHV) or Peak Growth Velocity (PVC) -
is a continuous variable, measured by age (in years) relative to the peak of
height in the period where maximum growth rate occurs. It's a
indication of maturity.

#### phv_state

Categorical description for PHV

#### these

Exercise Self-Efficacy Scale or ECE-AF — Confidence to Engage in
Physical Activity, measures how confident students are in relation to
to have physical activities and exercises. It is measured by a scale
psychometrics.

#### pmc

Perceived Motor Competence (PMC) or PCM — Perceived Competence
Motor, measures the child's own perception of their ability to
perform motor movements. It is measured by a psciometric scale.

#### kupa

Knowledge and Understanding of Physical Activity (KUPA) or ECC-AF —
Knowledge and Understanding of Physical Activity, assesses knowledge
about benefits, safety and participation in physical activities. AND
measured by scores on objective questions.

#### sb

Sedentary Behavior (SB) or Sedentary Behavior (CS). Evaluate the time
exposure to sedentary behaviors. It is measured by a scale
psychometrics.

#### shovel

Physical Activity (PA) or Physical Activity - Level of physical activity
of the child, based on frequency and duration of daily activities.
Measured by scores on a physical activity frequency questionnaire.

#### mc

Motor Competence - Supine-To-Stand, corresponds to motor competence
measured by the time (in seconds) it takes the child to get up from the
floor, from the supine position to the standing position.

#### tde

Reading Performance. Measures reading level based on time and
correct reading of words. Measured by Total Test Score
TDE.

#### tde_categorical

Categorical description of the TDE test.
