# rionegro_amazonas

A database of the level of the Rio Negro (Amazonas) from 2003 to 2023.

### Description

This database has data from the Rio Negro level, from the State of
Amazonas (Brazil), compiling information from years, semesters and days,
along with the river level in meters and whether it filled or leaked (in
centimeters).

### Usage


```r
rionegro_amazonas
```

### Format

‘rionegro_amazonas’ A data frame with 8003 lines and 7 columns:

#### level_m

River level in meters

#### increase_decrease_cm

Whether the river level has risen or fallen (in centimeters)

#### day

Measurement day

#### month

Month of measurement

#### year

Year of measurement

#### semester

Measurement semester
