# prf_amazonas

Accident data recorded by the Federal Highway Police in
Amazon in 2025

### Description

Records made by the Federal Highway Police about accidents that
took place in Amazonas in 2025

### Usage


```r
prf_amazonas
```

### Format

‘prf_amazonas’ A data frame with 116 lines and 30 variables:

####school

#### id

Record identifier

#### date

Registration date (yyyy-mm-dd)

#### days_of_week

Day of the week

#### team

Registration time

#### federative_unit

Federative unit (UF)

#### br

Federal Highway (BR)

#### km

Kilometer of BR

#### municipality

Municipality

#### accident_cause

Cause of the accident

#### accident_type

Type of accident

#### accident_classification

Accident classification: with victims, without victims, uninjured victims,
fatal victims

#### road_direction

Direction of the road

#### weather

Weather conditions at the time of the accident

#### track_type

Track type: Single, Multiple or Double

#### track_layout

Track layout

#### land_use

Land use

#### people

People involved in the accident

#### dead

Number of deaths

#### minor_injuries

Number of victims with minor injuries

#### serious_injuries

Number of victims with serious injuries

#### unharmed

Number of unharmed victims

#### ignored

Ignored

#### harmed

Number of injured victims

#### latitude

Latitude

#### longitude

Longitude

#### regional

Superintendence of the Federal Highway Police of Brazil

#### police_station

Police station where the registration was carried out

#### operating_unit

Operational unit and responsible police station
