# malaria_amazonas

An integrated database of malaria notifications in the state of
Amazon.

### Description

This database contains medical records of patients who underwent
screening tests for malaria in the Legal Amazon. The data from this
set were filtered to include only cases registered in the
state of Amazonas and patients who reside specifically in the state
from the Amazon.

### Usage


```r
malaria_amazonas
```

### Format

‘malaria_amazonas’ A data frame with 1098620 lines and 40 columns.

#### notification_state

Notification status

#### notification_rh

Notification health region

#### notification_county

Municipality of notification

#### infection_country

Probable municipality where the patient was infected

#### infection_state

Probable state where the patient was infected

#### infection_hr

Probable health region where the patient was infected

#### infection_county

Probable municipality where the patient was infected

#### home_country

Patient's country of residence

#### home_state

Patient's state of residence

#### home_hr

Patient's health region of residence

#### home_county

Patient's municipality of residence

#### exam_interval

Time interval between notification and examination

####treatment_interval

Time interval between the exam and the start of treatment

#### notification_interval

Time interval between symptoms and notification

#### notification_month

Month in which the notification was registered

#### notification_year

Year the notification was registered

#### symptom_month

Month in which the patient felt the first symptoms of malaria

#### symptom_year

Year in which the patient felt the first symptoms of malaria

#### exam_month

Month in which the exam was carried out

#### exam_year

Year the exam was taken

####treatment_month

Month in which treatment was started

####treatment_year

Year in which treatment was started

#### migration

Health region of residence different from that of notification

#### autochthonous_case

Health region of infection identical to that of residence

#### exam_result

Exam result

#### detection_type

Detection type

#### exam_type

Type of exam

#### symptoms

Indicates whether the patient felt any symptoms

#### hemiparasite

Test results for other hemiparasites

#### previous_treatment

Previous treatment for P. vivax or P. falciparum

#### occupation

Main activity in the last 15 days

#### education_level

Patient's education level

#### age

Patient age range

#### race

Patient's race/color

#### gender

Patient gender

#### pregnancy

Indicates pregnancy and gestational age

#### crosses

Indicates the amount of parasitemia in crosses

#### cvl_case

Indicates the existence of cases of canine visceral leishmaniasis

#### scheme

Indicates the treatment regimen used

#### qty_parasites

Indicates the number of parasites per mm³

#### month

Month in which this data was observed
